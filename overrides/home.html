Three things about that geometry are deliberate and easy to undo by accident: - The scanline grid runs 32/45/58/71, not 22/…/80. A bar laid across the baseline bisects the round terminals and leaves a four-unit crumb floating under every stem, which reads as a rendering fault rather than as a CRT.
