{{- if regexMatch "(?i)(nfs|efs|filestore|azure-?file|cephfs|smb|cifs|juicefs|gluster|lustre|s3|goofys)" (default "" .Values.persistence.storageClass) }}

WARNING: persistence.storageClass is {{ .Values.persistence.storageClass | quote }}, which looks
   network-backed. Mira mmaps every block it reads, and on a network filesystem
   an I/O hiccup arrives as SIGBUS — a signal, with nothing to catch and no way
   to unwind. Mira statfs()es the volume at startup and will refuse to run on
   NFS, SMB, CIFS, CephFS or 9P rather than die mid-query later. Use a local or
   block-backed class.
   (This warning is a match on the class *name* — a heuristic, and the startup
   check is the real gate. A local class named "efs-like" trips it harmlessly.)
{{- end }}
