The terminal UI says where its keys go. The key bar names the pane `esc` returns
to, sheds the second rank of keys on a narrow terminal, and marks a pane that
scrolls. An empty pane explains itself two ways — a directory with no blocks
points at `--data-dir`, a window with no matching rows points at `]` and `/` —
because those are different mistakes with different fixes. Help is grouped under
six headings by what a reader is trying to do.
